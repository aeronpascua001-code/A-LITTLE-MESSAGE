Happy Teacher's Month!

A little message of gratitude
🌷 ✦ 🌷

Dear Ma'am Jinky,

I just want to take this time to express how much I really appreciate you. You have been a great role model in our lives, a teacher I really look up upon. Thank you for giving us your time and care whenever we needed it the most. You have always been there in our saddest and happiest moments. You have been a part of our lives, a path that guided us to a better future. You have taught us many lessons: to be fair, to be humble, to be brave, and many, many more.

I hope this will not be the last time we'll see each other. I hope to meet you in ten years' time and make you proud, or I hope to also see you whenever it is possible. You have been there for us always, and I want to return the favor. If ever you need help, just call me and I'll do all that is in my power to help.

Once again, Happy Teacher's Month, and I want you to know that I really, really love you, my favourite second mother!!

From yours truly,
pinaka-poging anak ng Felipe!
